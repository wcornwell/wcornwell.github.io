A look at {{< pubspan >}} years of publications from a different angle than the [full list](/publications/): what the work is about, rather than what order it arrived in. Most of it is ecological data science — building and analysing large biodiversity datasets to get at environmental problems: fire, climate change, carbon cycling, and the loss of biodiversity in cities and beyond. It also tracks how the body of work has grown, and how my role in it has shifted from doing the research myself to helping run a group that does.
